# AWS Identity Compromise Investigation

> **Status: Planned lab.** This is a project outline, not a claim of a completed investigation.

## Objective
Construct a synthetic IAM credential misuse scenario and reconstruct the timeline from logs.

## Proposed tools
CloudTrail, IAM, GuardDuty

## Deliverables to publish
A synthetic CloudTrail JSON sample, investigation notebook or SPL queries, event timeline, IOC/TTP table, containment plan, and an executive summary.

## Suggested investigation report structure
1. Executive summary and scope
2. Initial hypothesis and data sources
3. Evidence collection and validation
4. Chronological event timeline
5. Findings and alternative explanations
6. MITRE ATT&CK mapping (where supported)
7. Containment and remediation recommendations
8. Detection opportunities and limitations
9. Reproducibility notes and references

## Safety and confidentiality
Use only lab systems and synthetic or public data. Never publish customer identifiers, internal logs, secrets, or employer-owned investigation content.
